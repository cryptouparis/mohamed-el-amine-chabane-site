01 / About

Networks that can make promises — and keep them.

I am a PhD researcher at CEA List and Institut Polytechnique de Paris / Télécom SudParis. My research lies at the intersection of deterministic networking and artificial intelligence, with the goal of making real-time industrial networks easier to configure, scale and validate.

My work studies Gate Control List scheduling for IEEE 802.1Qbv, reinforcement learning for network control, scalable multi-switch scheduling, and graph-based learning for topology-aware decision making.

02 / Research

Research directions

From exact timing guarantees to learning-based schedulers.

01

Time-Sensitive Networking

Deterministic Ethernet, IEEE 802.1Qbv Time-Aware Shaper, Gate Control Lists, end-to-end latency and jitter guarantees.

02

AI for Networks

Q-learning, deep reinforcement learning and policy optimization for automated network scheduling and control.

03

Scalable Scheduling

Multi-switch and multi-port scheduling methods designed to avoid combinatorial explosion as network size grows.

04

Graph Learning

Graph neural representations for topology-aware scheduling policies that can generalize across network instances.
